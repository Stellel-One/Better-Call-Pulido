# Better Call Pulido — Google Stitch & Visual Identity Guide

## 1. Visual Identity Spec
- **Concept:** Tacky, aggressive, hyper-commercial 2000s defense attorney website inspired by *Better Call Saul* (Albuquerque aesthetic meets local Colombian context).
- **Color Palette:**
  - `Parchment Yellow`: `#F4E8B0` / `#E8D588` (vintage aged paper background)
  - `Alert Red`: `#D91A1A` / `#B20D0D` (striking CTA, bold brush script titles)
  - `Navy / Black`: `#11161B` (condensed badges, strong contrast)
  - `Legal Gold`: `#F5B800` (stars, sparkles, badges)
- **Typography:**
  - Titles: Vintage 60s/70s brush script (resembling *Better Call Saul* logo) and impact/condensed all-caps font.
  - Body & Stamps: Bold condensed sans-serif with slight tilt, resembling rubber stamp prints.
- **Key Details:**
  - Phone: `320 4477463`
  - Motto: *"PULIDO • ABOGADO / ATTORNEY AT LAW"*
  - Sub-banner: *"LLAMA YA: 320 4477463 • SE HABLA ESPAÑOL"*
  - Domain target: `pulido.stellel.com`
